MOORLAND · THROUGH THE YEAR

Leave it better

Love the wild.
Look after it.

The best adventures are the ones that care for the places we come to enjoy. A little preparation goes a long way.

↗

Read the weather

Conditions can change quickly on open moorland. Check the forecast and visibility before you leave.

⌖

Know your way

Carry a suitable map and compass or reliable navigation device, and know how to use them.

⌁

Pack for the place

Wear sturdy footwear and layers suited to the terrain, forecast and length of your walk.

♡

Respect the living landscape

Follow current access guidance and restrictions. Give ponies and livestock space, keep dogs under control, and protect fragile habitats.

◷

Plan within your limits

Choose a route that suits your ability and daylight hours. Tell someone your plans for longer walks.

Before your visit, check current guidance from Dartmoor National Park Authority ↗.
